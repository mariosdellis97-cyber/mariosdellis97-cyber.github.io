My story

From Greek stages to London gigs

  • GreecePlaying guitar and bass at lots of venues
  • 2018 – 2021BSc Music Technology, University of Hertfordshire: songwriting and music production
  • The last 10 yearsGigging across London and the UK with many bands and projects
  • 5+ releasesIncluding Velvet Grip's singles in 2025 and 2026, where I composed, recorded and performed on bass

I started out in Greece, playing guitar and bass at lots of venues. In the UK I studied Music Technology at the University of Hertfordshire, where my degree focused on songwriting and music production.

For the past ten years I have been gigging across London and around the UK with all sorts of bands and projects: acoustic and electric, rock and pop. Guitar and bass are my main instruments, and I also play some drums and keys.

I have more than five official releases to my name. Most recently I composed, recorded and performed on bass for Velvet Grip's singles Highway Lights and Wind Chimes. Both are out on Spotify, Apple Music and Amazon Music, and their official videos have passed 29,000 views on YouTube between them.

Why I want to teach

Teaching kids to play rock music has been my dream since I was a kid myself.

Music has given me more than ten years of gigs, bands and songs. I want to pass that passion on, along with everything I have learnt on stage, and inspire the next generation of musicians.

The moment I would love to help a child reach is simple: picking up an instrument for the first time, then playing it with their band in front of an audience.

  • Coaching experience from supervising hospitality teams
  • Full UK driving licence and my own SUV for band equipment
  • Committed to safeguarding; happy to complete an enhanced DBS check
  • Fluent in English and Greek
